Operational Context

New York Infrastructure Mission

For public utility grids balancing megawatts across millions of AMI smart meters and financial institutions processing volatile market order streams, unpredictable model latency is fatal. This summit tears down the hardware and networking optimizations required to achieve sub-50ms Time-To-First-Token and deterministic p99 latency boundaries at institutional scale.

Strictly capped at 40 participants holding direct technical authority over forward deployment engineering, sovereign GPU superclusters, or enterprise production topology. Zero sales decks. Raw telemetry traces, latency benchmarks, and live architecture reviews.

Technical Agenda

Session 01 Concurrency & Decoding

Sub-50ms TTFT: Paged KV-Cache & Speculative Decoding

Deep dive into multi-GPU speculative decoding architectures. Benchmarking dynamic draft models, flash-attention adaptations, and chunked prefill strategies under concurrent loads exceeding 10,000 requests/sec.

Session 02 Industrial Telemetry

SCADA & Power Grid Ingestion at 10-Second Continuous Loops

Splicing frontier reasoning models directly into private utility VPCs (Con Edison, Shell). Ingesting continuous harmonic telemetry across 5M+ smart meters to preempt substation transformer overload and optimize routing.

Session 03 Execution Correctness

Formal Verification of Generated Model Outputs

Employing runtime mathematical assertion harnesses to validate every token sequence before it executes against institutional databases, automated trading gateways, or electrical grid switching hardware.
