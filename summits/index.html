What happens at a summit

  1. Step 1

    Use-case discovery

    Operations, engineering and plant leaders map their biggest problems, such as downtime, quality, energy and safety, and rank where AI can help first.

  2. Step 2

    Data readiness

    A practical look at the data each use case needs: what is usable today, what is missing and how to close the gap.

  3. Step 3

    Prototypes and a pilot plan

    STAGE-X Residency teams demo prototypes built on real use cases, and each company leaves with a plan for a first pilot.
