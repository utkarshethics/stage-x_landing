01 / WHY THIS CITY
FOR MANUFACTURING, OIL & GAS AND ENERGY COMPANIES

Berlin Use-Case Mission

Germany's automotive and machinery makers run some of the most automated plants in the world, with long supplier chains behind them. This summit looks at AI use cases that raise first-pass quality on the line and give earlier warning of supplier and parts disruptions.

Each summit is capped at 40 participants: operations, engineering, plant and digital leaders from manufacturing, oil & gas and energy companies, alongside STAGE-X engineers. No sales decks. Real use cases, the data behind them, and working prototypes from STAGE-X Residency teams.

02 / USE CASES ON THE TABLE

Use-Case Sessions

December 5–6, 2026 • Full-Day Program

  1. Use Case 01 QUALITY

    In-Line Inspection on Assembly Lines

    Vision models that check welds, paint and assembly at line speed and send each defect back to the right station.

  2. Use Case 02 MAINTENANCE

    Robot & Press Health Monitoring

    Predicting failures in welding robots, presses and conveyors from controller and sensor data.

  3. Use Case 03 SUPPLY CHAIN

    Early Warning for Parts Shortages

    Reading supplier, logistics and ERP data to flag parts at risk weeks before they stop a line.
