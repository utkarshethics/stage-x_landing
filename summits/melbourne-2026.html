Roundtable Thesis

Operational Context

Melbourne Infrastructure Mission

Australia's mining and natural resources operators lead the world in heavy autonomous machinery. This roundtable gathers engineering leaders deploying ruggedized edge servers directly into autonomous haulage fleets, remote open-pit mine sites, and sub-surface seismic modeling workflows operating under extreme environmental isolation.

Strictly capped at 40 participants holding direct technical authority over forward deployment engineering, sovereign GPU superclusters, or enterprise production topology. Zero sales decks. Raw telemetry traces, latency benchmarks, and live architecture reviews.

Deep Teardowns

Technical Agenda

Session 01 Autonomous Fleet Ops

Zero-Latency Edge Inference on Autonomous Haulage Trucks

Deploying vibration-hardened inference modules on 400-ton autonomous haul trucks. Managing real-time obstacle avoidance, path arbitration, and tele-operation failover.

Session 02 Geotechnical Modeling

High-Throughput 3D Seismic Inversion & Strata Modeling

Running multimodal frontier foundation models on 3D geological strata telemetry to predict slope failure, rockburst risk, and ore purity in active mining pits.

Session 03 Satellite Edge Topology

Isolated Mine Site Edge Clusters: Zero-WAN Operational Survival

Designing self-healing local micro-clusters that maintain continuous autonomous mine site operations during satellite blackouts or weather disruptions.
