Operational Context

Dubai Infrastructure Mission

The Gulf region is deploying the world's most aggressive sovereign compute and energy infrastructure initiatives. This closed-door session explores the deployment of sovereign frontier model clusters powering upstream energy operations, extreme-heat data center engineering, and autonomous utility microgrids.

Strictly capped at 40 participants holding direct technical authority over forward deployment engineering, sovereign GPU superclusters, or enterprise production topology. Zero sales decks. Raw telemetry traces, latency benchmarks, and live architecture reviews.
