HOW IT WORKS

Real industry problems. Real audience.

Each city's sprint runs for 1–2 weeks and ends on that city's summit weekend, in front of the companies whose use cases you worked on.

  1. STEP 01

    Register

    Select your host city and register your team (1–4 builders).

  2. STEP 02

    Build

    Pick a real use case from the manufacturing, oil & gas and energy companies attending your city’s summit, and build a prototype in person over 1–2 weeks.

  3. STEP 03

    Demo

    Finalists present their prototypes to those companies at the STAGE-X Summit weekend in their city.

  4. STEP 04

    Win

    Compete for the $5 Million YC Runway prize pool.
