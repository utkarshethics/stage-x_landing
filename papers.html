Context, before you read the numbers
47 days
Median time from signed engagement to first production inference, across our last six deployments. The range is wide; the slowest took more than twice that.
$100M+ sourcing spend
Client-reported savings from our pricing-discrepancy audit work. Not independently audited, and concentrated in a few large engagements rather than spread evenly.
99.2% telemetry uptime
Aggregate across managed fleets over the trailing twelve months. Individual customers saw 98% to 99.9% depending on maintenance windows we did not control.
<50ms first token
The target we design to for grid and trading workloads, and roughly what we measure at p50. Tail latency sits higher; the TTFT papers show the full distribution.
