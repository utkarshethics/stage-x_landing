Forty seats. No pitches.

01

Strict Chatham House Rule

Participants are free to use the information and code strategies received, but neither the identity nor the affiliation of the speaker or any participant may be revealed. Absolute operational candor is required.

02

Zero Slide Decks & Pitches

No vendor pitches, marketing narratives, or high-level hypotheticals. Every session centers on architecture diagrams, kernel optimization profiles, latency traces, and live infrastructure code.

Strictly capped at 40 participants per summit. Attendees must hold direct technical authority over mission-critical enterprise compute, forward deployment teams, or frontier model engineering stacks.

Architecture Teardown Sessions

Every session centers on architecture diagrams, kernel optimization profiles, latency traces, and live infrastructure code.

  1. 01
    MODEL QUANTIZATION & KERNEL TUNING

    FP8 / FP4 Serving Without Accuracy Degradation

    Deep dive into real-world benchmarks contrasting native FP16 execution with vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, and SGLang FP8 kernels across enterprise reasoning workloads. Live telemetry on GPU memory pressure and cache eviction.

  2. 02
    ADVERSARIAL HARDENING

    Eliminating Non-Deterministic Hallucinations in Critical Ops

    Hard-constraint decoding, grammar-constrained sampling, and formal verification frameworks for frontier model outputs deployed in defense, energy grid balancing, and predictive healthcare pipelines.

  3. 03
    ENTERPRISE VPC TOPOLOGY

    Zero-Trust Cross-Boundary Frontier Model Gateway

    Reference architectures for embedding OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and open-weight models into multi-tenant VPCs with hardware security modules (HSMs) and zero plain-text data logging.

    TCO & MODEL GOVERNANCE

    Frontier Open Source vs. Closed Lab APIs: The 2026 Audit

    Comprehensive capital expenditure and operational expenditure audit: training/fine-tuning cluster costs vs. token-based cloud bills at 100M+ tokens/day scale with full IP ownership.
