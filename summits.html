01 / ROUNDTABLE PROTOCOLS

Forty seats. No pitches.

01

Strict Chatham House Rule

Participants are free to use the information and code strategies received, but neither the identity nor the affiliation of the speaker or any participant may be revealed. Absolute operational candor is required.

02

Zero Slide Decks & Pitches

No vendor pitches or marketing narratives. Every session starts from a real operational problem: downtime, quality, energy, safety. It ends with a use case and the data it needs.

03

Industry Leaders Only

Strictly capped at 40 participants per summit: operations, engineering, plant and digital leaders from manufacturing, oil & gas and energy companies, alongside STAGE-X engineers.

02 / WHO ATTENDS

Three industries. One question.

Where should AI go to work first in our plants, fields and grids? Every summit is built around that question, with use cases matched to the industries strongest in each city.

Manufacturing

Automotive, machinery, steel, chemicals and materials makers looking for AI that holds up on the shop floor.

EXAMPLE USE CASES
  • Vision-based quality inspection
  • Predictive maintenance on lines and robots
  • Energy use per unit of output
  • Early warning of supply disruptions
Focus cities: Boston, Toronto, Berlin

Oil & Gas

Upstream producers, offshore operators, refineries and petrochemical complexes with large, remote or hazardous assets.

EXAMPLE USE CASES
  • Well and production optimisation
  • Pipeline leak and corrosion detection
  • Drone and subsea inspection review
  • Furnace, boiler and equipment reliability
Focus cities: London, Dubai, Mumbai

Energy & Utilities

Power producers, grid operators, renewables and storage companies, and mining operations with heavy energy needs.

EXAMPLE USE CASES

05 / THE DAY

From problem to pilot in one day

Every summit follows the same four sessions, focused on the industries and use cases of that city.

  1. 01
    USE-CASE DISCOVERY

    Where AI Pays Off in Your Operations

    Plant, field and control-room leaders map their biggest operational problems (downtime, quality, energy, safety) and rank where AI can help first.

  2. 02
    DATA READINESS

    What Data Each Use Case Needs

    A practical look at historians, SCADA, maintenance systems and ERP data: what is usable today, what is missing, and how to close the gap.

  3. 03
    RESIDENCY DEMOS

    Prototypes Built on Real Use Cases

    STAGE-X Residency teams present the prototypes they built during the 1–2 week sprint, around use cases from the companies in the room.

  4. 04
    FROM USE CASE TO PILOT

    Planning a Pilot That Reaches Production

    Scoping a first pilot: how success is measured, the team it needs, security and safety requirements, and how to take it from one site to many.
